# TOHEŞAR Miniature Collection 01 — BUILD 006

Art direction: fine automotive illustration, compact collectible proportions, precise silver wheels, smoked glass, graphite contours and lilac/peach pearl highlights. 005's Lilac Coupé is the style anchor and remains untouched.

- `wedge-source.png`: new coral vermilion wedge, low nose, dark intake and high rear wing.
- `gt-source.png`: new sage silver grand tourer, long hood, rearward cabin and fine chrome detailing.
- Existing anchor: `../journal-world-005/miniature-source.png`.

New works were made with AI image generation using the preserved coupe as a reference, then visually reviewed together. These are brand-directed illustrations, not licensed manufacturer drawings, exact production-model diagrams, documentary photographs or manufacturer collaborations. No manufacturer logo was requested or added. The shapes draw from automotive design history; no claim of registered industrial-design exclusivity is made.

Sources retain their full resolution and alpha. `scripts/optimize_miniatures_006.py` makes trimmed 1200px transparent WebP derivatives for all three works in `journal/assets/miniatures/`. The original 005 source and WebP are never overwritten. The WebP exports are rendering assets, not independent new artworks.

# TOHEŞAR — Cover Art Max 004

Based on Cover 003 at `416387249964a9bdadfa089893f3bdf8b4c094cb`. Isolated design study; no production deployment.

The original wordmark sits inside the photographed sky. The same photograph opens into a plate while the staggered Korean headline follows its lower edge. The cedilla enters only after the upper margin clears.

Serve the repository root and open `/creative/cover-art-max-004/`. Original SVGs, photo and local fonts are referenced without alteration. To replace the photo, update both URLs in `index.html`, the focus in `photo.css`, and framing limits in `cover.js`.

`previews/`: actual Chromium iPhone-proportion renders at 390 × 844, covering 0%, 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% of the scroll sequence. iPad and desktop compositions were also visually reviewed. No generated photography or scroll interception.

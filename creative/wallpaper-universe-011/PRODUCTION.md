# TOHEŞAR / Wallpaper Universe 011

Eight commissioned AI-assisted automotive illustrations, generated 2026-10-29
with the session's native ImageGen tool. No existing photograph or 005–010
miniature was used as an input. `originals/collection-source.png` is the untouched
1536 × 1024 transparent generation. The eight individual PNGs preserve its cells
(two columns, four rows, each 768 × 256). All originals remain available.

Direction: sophisticated Y2K automotive shirt-print / airbrush illustration;
soft plum contours, pearly highlights, smoky blue glass, delicate silver wheels.
Lilac, coral, sage, powder blue, butter, rose, tomato and mint form one palette.
Eight different body proportions were requested together to establish one hand:
round coupe, wedge, long GT, saloon, hatch, flowing coupe, roadster and wagon.
The collection was inspected on the actual warm editorial paper before use.
The motifs evoke recognizable automotive design traditions; they are neither
measured drawings nor manufacturer-endorsed products. The small generated
details are illustration, not evidence of a particular vehicle specification.

The generation brief requested fully separated vehicles, transparent gutters,
no text, no sticker outlines, no photographic cutouts, and no scene background.
Reference research informed only general print language, not copied artwork:

- https://www.tokyotoms.com/collections/tokyo-toms-t-shirts/products/gt86-anime-livery-t-shirt
- https://importcrate.com/collections/kaiga-graphic-series
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbrush_Action_Magazine

No third-party reference image was incorporated. Existing site photo licences,
credits and AI visual-fiction disclosures remain unchanged. AI assistance and
vehicle illustration status are disclosed in the article's existing image note
and the public 011 review. Generated assets are not represented as human-drawn
or exclusive licensed manufacturer assets. Any later merchandise production
requires its own brand/rights and print-resolution review.

The asset registry is `journal/data/wallpaper-artworks.json`. Intentional
coordinates and motion values live in `journal/data/wallpaper-layouts.json`.
`scripts/optimize_wallpaper_011.cjs` exports 240/480px transparent WebPs from the
individual PNGs. The larger primary WebPs were tightly framed with the same
alpha trim (threshold 20), quality 90 / alpha quality 100. Small responsive
derivatives improve WebKit's downsampling of rotated motifs as well as transfer
size. Node, Sharp and Playwright are development tools, not site dependencies.

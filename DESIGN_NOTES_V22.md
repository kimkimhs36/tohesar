# TOHEŞAR V2.2 — European Motoring Editorial

Art direction: high-brow European motoring newspaper with carefully balanced rhythm between heritage photojournalism and modern motion; visibly edited content, not brand self-promotion. The prototype remains `noindex`.

**Design changes**
- Photographic cover designed as a coherent full-width editorial spread, with the complete uncropped F40 vehicle photograph and clear attribution.
- Hero cover now gives title, actual car history and photography first place, no philosophical slogans or disconnected UI.
- Tailored Hangul editorial serif system, softer paper/ink, oxblood accent used sparingly; deliberate use of Bodoni for the TOHEŞAR masthead only.
- Newsroom date and reporting sources retained; proper photo label clearly identifies 1984 Espace as archival reference rather than the 2026 concept.
- Deep long-form reading with robust hierarchy, elegant headline and chapter spacing, accessible text sizing.
- Graceful entrance transitions, 900ms photo easing, no hover-induced layout shift, reduced-motion support, no animations hiding editorial text.
- If external photo fails, visually considered typographic placeholders rather than empty grey rectangles. Only licensed real photo sources; no old TOHEŞAR art or AI images.

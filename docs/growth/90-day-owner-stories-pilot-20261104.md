# TOHEŞAR / 90-DAY CULTURE GROWTH PILOT
**Decision log · 2026-11-04 → 2027-02-04**
**Status:** execution blueprint, no follower-growth guarantee; no changes to production main.

## Objective
Turn TOHEŞAR from a beautiful automotive web experiment into a recognizable name among real automotive owners and photographers. Primary outcome: strangers willingly submit/accept vehicle features, share their personal TOHEŞAR page, return and recommend. Product income is secondary this quarter.

## Strategic bet
**OWNER STORIES** = a highly designed, original, photo-led profile of one person's car and their aesthetic decisions, publishable as a single shareable mobile URL. The featured owner has a natural incentive to share it. Unlike mass sticker giveaways, one approved owner feature generates useful editorial material + authentic distribution + a collaborator relationship.

## Non-negotiable
- Existing GitHub main, site logo, SEO, publishing and experimental work preserved. Preview branch is not a published live service.
- iPhone Safari first; iPad Safari responsive landscape; desktop functional. Editorial real photography, light peach/mint accents, restrained motion, accessibility, low data weight.
- Only source images with rights and photographer attribution. Never invent interviews, testimonials, car specs, owner's identity or results.
- No spray-and-pray spam; no unauthorized placement on people's cars. Photos and direct quotes require clear written consent + publication review + ability to request correction.
- 17-year-old operator: meet new contacts in public during daytime with a trusted friend/adult; avoid unsafe night meetups. Never disclose private home address.
- No spending on ads, inventory, sponsorship promises or manufacturing commitments without budget approval.

## The funnel
1. Discover: original short-form owner photo (Reels, account tag, self-published journal link)
2. Want: genuinely beautiful mobile TOHEŞAR profile, 20–30 seconds to understand
3. Participate: opt-in via owner-stories page email CTA
4. Share: owner receives a clean 9:16 story preview and a direct owner-story URL
5. Return: credible archive of people, modifications and aesthetic choices
6. Earn: sticker / apparel offer after credible demand, not before.

## Key metrics (collect starting baselines, no fabricated rates)
- Outreach log: attempts / replies / acceptances / publish permissions / links shared / reposts
- Content log: original stories published, owner-approved photos, metadata, attribution, publication date
- Site: referral visits tagged by link, returning readers where privacy-respecting analytics support it, story-read rate
- Product: quoted sample price, paid orders, shipped goods, owners voluntarily displaying sticker
- Core ratio: published stories per 10 targeted invitations; owner reposts per story (more meaningful than follower count alone).

## Audience test
Priority: 1. Small highly aesthetic owners (OEM+, elegant older European/Japanese/Korean coupes). 2. Independent car photographers. 3. Local owners clubs and editorial pages. 4. Event organizers (only after producing strong sample feature). Huge influencer accounts and giveaways are low priority.
Do not pay for audience or buy followers.

## Ready-to-publish gate
Before pushing any public page: image attribution, CTA email working, correct source permission and narrative, responsive QA on iPhone, thumbnail/OG preview, no broken links, no aggressive email collection; approval of final design. No merge to main unless reviewed.

## Design learning
Current `index.html` already has cinematic hero, static journal, indexed SEO and editorial assets. Growth should build an *owner participation + shareability layer*, not tear out proven architecture. Run as small separate path then optionally add unobtrusive CTA to cover/journal upon editorial approval.

## Ownership / focus
Execution is possible in chat immediately. Ongoing automated checks may monitor sticker quotes, but site changes, user interviews, real-world handoffs and spend require explicit tools/permissions. This is a goals framework, not a claim that any collaboration has occurred.

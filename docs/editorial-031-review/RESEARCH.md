# Editorial research and verification — 031

Reviewed 3 November 2026, including phone-width browser renders:

- [Type 7: Silver Bullets](https://type7.com/blogs/stories/silver-bullets) uses an unframed photograph, a direct title and a narrow, comfortably spaced column. Its text and photographs take turns in the scroll. Applied here: photographic continuity, a quieter column and captions immediately beside their images; not its condensed Latin typeface or store UI.
- [Awwwards: SavoirFaire©](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/savoirfaire-c/) identifies the project as Site of the Day on 19 March 2024; [the actual studio site](https://www.savoirfaire.nyc/) uses a high-contrast typographic scale and generous black space. Applied here: clear hierarchy and deliberate pauses. Its strong motion and oversized display treatment were not carried into article copy.
- [CSS Design Awards: Atelier Migliore](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/atelier-migliore/50635/) was shown as a **nominee** at inspection, not as a verified winner. The listing's monochrome photographic composition and restrained framing informed the reduction of background decoration. Its nomination listing was reviewed; access to the linked site itself was not available in this session.

The baseline and revised Chromium captures use the same viewport, scroll target and reduced-motion setting. Comparison sheets show opening and body, before and after, for F40, Giulia and Jaguar. A 1180 × 820 viewport represents iPad landscape dimensions; it is not a hardware screenshot.

# Journal & ISSUE — visual iterations

Source: `scripts/build_beauty_editorial.py` and `journal/assets/beauty014-editorial.css`.

- `pass1-390-*` and `pass1-1440-*`: revised Journal, articles and ISSUE chapters (recaptured after correcting the shared photo veil).
- `pass2-*`: intermediate 430 / 820 / 1180 compositions, before correcting the shared photo veil. Retained as iteration evidence.
- `pass3-*`: second visual pass at 430 × 932, 820 × 1180, 1180 × 820, after veil correction.
- `pass4-*-issue-curve`: final curve chapter with its reading mask matched to the lavender paper.

The Journal now pairs a tightly tracked sans masthead with a peach headline slip that overlaps the blue-hour image. The car is substantially larger within its photo, and its whole silhouette remains inside the plate. The five existing ISSUE chapters retain their sequence and copy but differentiate their forms: curved 911, sharply set F40, long Giulia profile, wide blue Type 01, and three miniature silhouettes at different angles. The standalone Giulia opening was already strong and is preserved.

The second pass removed excess sky from the desktop Type 01 plate while retaining the whole car. The shared stylesheet fixes a credit pseudo-element that had unintentionally veiled photographs. A final lavender paper correction removes beige clouds around the 911 text.

All 50 viewport/page combinations were rendered in Chromium, with no horizontal overflow. The visible text of all seven pages was compared with the base commit and remained identical. These are browser emulations; physical iPhone/iPad Safari has not been tested.

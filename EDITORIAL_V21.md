# TOHEŞAR / DESIGN AND EDITORIAL V2.1

Independent refinement of the newspaper-inspired V2 prototype. V1 / old Yokohama and Memory Alloy code and imagery are excluded. This is a separate preview branch, not tohesar.com production.

## Editorial and UX improvements
- Actual headline and dated news surfaced before excessive brand prose. No fake rolling news feed.
- Full image proportions retained: no forced crop on lead/article vehicle photos.
- No duplicate F40 or Renault photos on the homepage archive tiles.
- Editorial story chapters and functional in-page navigation on desktop.
- User-selectable reading text sizing 90%–120%, saved locally for that browser. No unexplained save counter.
- Explicit, tap-to-open full-image viewer; Esc and close button work.
- Reduced-motion support and restrained page transitions.
- Four full static articles with source links, accurately distinguishing a 1984 archive Renault from a 2026 E-Space concept.

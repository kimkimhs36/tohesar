## Reproducible use
1. Open SVG in a vector-capable browser/editor. Print on A4 at **100% / actual size**, with "Fit to page" OFF.
2. Physically measure the calibration bar; if it is not exactly 100 mm, **discard the paper** and fix print scaling. No physical paper printing or measurement was performed in this cloud run.
3. Cut out the full 82 × 18.86 mm rectangle; tape to a blank charcoal garment at the desired left-chest area.
4. Record actual garment size, CF-to-art-left (CF=center front), HPS-to-art-top (HPS=high point shoulder), actual flat chest and placement acceptance. **No position is prescribed as supplier-approved.**
5. This only validates physical *relative* appearance, not printer printable zones, underbase, opacity, ink durability, wash behavior, exact vendor logo-inclusive quote, or approval.

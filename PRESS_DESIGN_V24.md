# TOHEŞAR V2.4 — Newspaper reading & motion

Scope: article interiors only. Existing approved home layout and iPad cover are kept intact.

## Improvements
- Compact, carefully typeset headlines and continuous editorial paragraphs; substantial reduction in previous oversized article headings.
- 4 researched, verified facts per article in a hairline-based press facts strip, never pill-cards.
- Additional reporting context using primary manufacturer references for Ferrari F40, Porsche 911, Ducati 916 and Renault E-Space Concept.
- Clear year/date, category, issue and authorship byline, plus verified manufacturer link.
- Explicit historical-photo label for Renault 1984 archive photograph (not presented as 2026 concept).
- Cautious native animation, scroll-reveal for article sections on supporting devices and accessibility-friendly reduced motion.
- Retain working search, adjustable font size, TOC, reading progress, photo zoom, sharing.

## Critical launch checks
- News feed and article publishing system are still manual, not automated.
- All images from externally hosted websites require licensing, attribution and browser compatibility re-check prior to production.
- Noindex and robots disallow must remain enabled during prototyping.
- Browser-based visual verification must be performed on real Safari iPhone & iPad; static structural and CSS tests are not substitutes.
